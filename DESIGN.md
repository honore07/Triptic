# DESIGN.md - Alpine Heritage & Old Modernity System

## 1. Core Visual Vibe
- **Concept:** 19th-century scientific expedition journals, Edward Whymper-style rock engravings, and botanical archives meeting high-end contemporary digital design.
- **Rule:** Zero generic AI gradients, heavy shadows, or saturated colors. Favor editorial austerity, stark contrast, and rich textures.

## 2. Color Palette (Tokens)
- `--color-bg`: #F4F1EA (Warm aged paper / parchment)
- `--color-text`: #111111 (Deep carbon ink black)
- `--color-surface`: #EFECE6 (Subtle muted container fill)
- `--color-accent`: #3A4A3F (Muted alpine pine / forest slate)
- `--color-border`: #222222 (Fine crisp structural line)

## 3. Typography
- **Display / Headers:** Cormorant Garamond or high-contrast Editorial Serif (Uppercase or expressive title case, tracked with precision).
- **Body / Utility:** Inter or Space Grotesk (Clean, geometric, highly legible sans-serif for modern contrast).
- **Code / Mono:** JetBrains Mono (For data, technical notes, or coordinates).

## 4. Component Guidelines
- **Borders & Dividers:** Always use thin 1px solid solid lines (`--color-border`). No soft rounded iOS-style cards.
- **Illustrations / Heroes:** Line art, cross-hatching, high-contrast monochrome engravings, topographic map lines.
- **Layout:** Generous whitespace, asymmetric grids, strict editorial alignment, structured framing reminiscent of printed plates.
